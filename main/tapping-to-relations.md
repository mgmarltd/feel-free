## Part 1 — EFT + NLP Therapy Structure

Template for an EFT + NLP session, modeled on the *Fear of Rejection* sample structure. Substitute the issue, body location, and matching affirmation from the library in Part 3 for any other problem.


### 1. Identify the Problem & Measure Intensity

Ask the client to describe the problem in one sentence:

> “I’m always scared people will reject me.”

> “I panic when someone might say no to me.”

> “I change myself to please people because I’m afraid they’ll leave.”

Clarify where it shows up most:

> “In which situations do you feel this fear of rejection the most?”

Romantic relationships?

Friendships / social groups?

Family?

Work (boss / colleagues / clients)?

Dating / texting / social media?

> “What exactly triggers it?”

No reply to a message?

Someone’s tone, facial expression?

Criticism?

Silence / distance?

Ask for the core belief behind it:

> “What do you unconsciously believe when you feel this fear?”

> “I’m not enough.”

> “I’m not lovable.”

> “There is something wrong with me.”

> “If they reject me, it means I’m worthless.”

SUD level (0–10):

“When you imagine being rejected right now (for example, someone you care about leaving, ignoring, or saying no), how strong is the fear?
0–10, where 10 is the worst you can imagine.”

You will come back to this number several times.


### 2. Locate the Fear Energy in the Body

Turn the fear into energy, not “who I am.”

> “Where do you feel this fear of rejection most in or around your body?”

Chest, heart area, stomach/solar plexus, throat, face, back of the neck, aura around the body, etc.

Ask for details:

> “If this fear were a form of energy, what would it be?”

Solid, liquid, gas, fog, smoke, cold stone, tight rope, metal cage, etc.

> “What color is it?”

> “What shape does it have?”

> “Is it still or moving? Heavy or light? Hot or cold? Tight or loose?”

You’ll use this in tapping:

> “this heavy dark pressure in my chest,”
“this tight cold knot in my stomach,” etc.


### 3. EFT Tapping Round – Fear of Rejection

Explain that you will tap together through the points.
Use your regular sequence. Here’s a ready script modeled on your sample structure.

5–7 gentle taps per phrase.

Ask them to breathe at each point: Inhale… Exhale…


#### i. Top of the Head (TOH) – (Inhale – Exhale)

> “I feel this fear of rejection right now.”
> “This is just energy in my energy body / energy field.”
> “This is just fear-of-rejection energy in my body.”
> “Even though I have this fear energy, I choose to let it flow out of my energy body / field.”
“Even though this fear is strong, there were times when I felt loved and accepted,
so I know this fear is temporary and I can let it go when I choose.”
> “I love and approve of myself.”
> “I trust the flow of life.”
> “I am safe and secure, even when someone says no to me.”


#### ii. Forehead / Third Eye (Between Eyebrows) – (Inhale – Exhale)

> “This is just energy in my energy body / field.”
> “This is just fear-of-rejection energy in my body, and I can let it move.”
> “Even though this fear is here, it’s okay, because I’ve felt loved and calm before without it.”
> “This means this fear is temporary and it can go away when I wish.”
> “I now choose to release this fear-of-rejection energy from my energy body / field.”
> “I love and approve of myself.”
> “I trust the flow of life.”
> “I am safe and secure, even if someone doesn’t like me.”


#### iii. Side of the Head / Temples – (Inhale – Exhale)

> “I know there have been many times I felt this fear of rejection.”
> “And I also know it has always passed.”
> “Every time it came, eventually it went.”
> “So I know it will pass this time too.”
> “I love and approve of myself.”
> “I trust the flow of life.”
> “I am safe and secure, even when I feel emotionally vulnerable.”


#### iv. Under the Eyes – (Inhale – Exhale)

> “I know there have been many times I felt this way.”
> “And I know it passed, and it will pass again.”
> “Now I take control of this fear energy and let it flow out of my body / field with relaxation.”
> “I am capable of feeling safe, even when someone doesn’t choose me.”
> “My mind and my body are wired to overcome this.”
> “I love and approve of myself.”
> “I trust the flow of life.”
> “I am safe and secure, even when people have their own opinions about me.”


#### v. Under the Nose – (Inhale – Exhale)

> “I let this fear-of-rejection energy go and free myself from it.”
> “I choose self-respect instead of panic.”
> “I choose to stay kind to myself, even when others say no.”
> “I love and approve of myself.”
> “I trust the flow of life.”
> “I am safe and secure, even when I feel exposed and vulnerable.”


#### vi. Chin – (Inhale – Exhale)

> “I let this fear-of-rejection energy go and free myself from it.”
> “I choose to stand in my worth, no matter how others respond.”
> “I love and approve of myself.”
> “I trust the flow of life.”
> “I am safe and secure while I show my true self.”


#### vii. Chest / Heart – (Inhale – Exhale)

> “I let this fear energy go and I free myself from it.”
> “I open space in my heart for love, acceptance and inner security.”
> “I love and approve of myself exactly as I am.”
> “I trust the flow of life.”
> “I am safe and secure in my body, even when someone rejects me or walks away.”


### 4. Re-Assess the Problem

Ask again:

> “When you imagine being rejected now (someone leaving, saying no, ignoring you), what number is the fear? (0–10)”

Note the new number.

a) If intensity dropped but not to zero

Example: from 9 → 5, or 8 → 3.

Acknowledge: “Good, it moved.”

Do a shorter round focusing on remaining fear:

> “Even though I still have some remaining fear of rejection…”
> “I release the remaining fear from my energy body / field…”

Repeat until it reaches 0–2.

b) If it feels the same / still very high (7–10)

Move into EFT + NLP combo:

> “Fear-of-rejection entity”

root memories (childhood rejection, bullying, emotional neglect, breakups, abandonment).


### 5. EFT + NLP Deep Work (If Fear Still Feels Strong)

#### 5.1 Ask: “Is this fear stronger than you?”

> “When you imagine this fear of rejection as energy, does it feel bigger and stronger than you?”

If YES, continue.


#### 5.1.a Turn Fear of Rejection into an Entity

Shape the entity:

> “If this fear were a being or a creature in front of you, how would it look?”

Size, shape, color, movement, facial expression.

NLP submodality play:

> “With each deep breath, imagine this fear entity moving further away from you.”
— or —

> “With each breath, you become bigger, taller, brighter, and this fear gets smaller and weaker.”

While the client visualizes, tap gently on the Chest / Heart and say:

> “I created you with my thoughts and experiences.”
> “I raised you by repeating old memories and painful stories.”
> “Now I choose to free you from my energy body.”
> “You were created by my thoughts, and I release you with love and peace.”
> “I am safe without you, and it is okay to let you go now.”
> “I am bigger than this fear.”
> “My heart, my worth, and my true self are stronger than this old program.”

Guide the release:

> “Watch this entity shrink to half its size… half again… and again… until it becomes a tiny dot.”
> “Let that dot dissolve into light and completely leave your field.”

Re-check intensity (0–10).


#### 5.2 Clear the Root Memory (First/Worst Rejection)

If fear is still present or you want deeper transformation, go to the root.

Ask:

> “What is the first or worst memory of feeling rejected, abandoned or not chosen?”

Possible roots:

A parent emotionally distant, leaving, or preferring a sibling

Being bullied / excluded at school

A first love or important partner leaving

Being cheated on or ghosted

Being told “you’re not good enough”

Have them step into the memory:

> “Close your eyes.”

> “Put your hands on your chest.”

> “Take three deep breaths and be there for a moment.”

Ask:

> “How old are you there?”

> “What exactly is happening?”

> “What is this younger you feeling?”

Unlovable, not enough, abandoned, ashamed, deeply hurt.

Tap while they are in that scene:

> “This younger me feels so rejected.”
> “This younger me feels not good enough.”
> “This younger me feels unloved and unimportant.”
“Even though I felt deeply rejected, I deeply love and approve of myself now.” (bringing in self-approval from your affirmation table )
> “That was one painful moment in time, not my whole identity.”
> “I survived it; I grew from it.”
> “Now I send love, protection and strength to this younger me.”
> “I reclaim my worth.”
> “I reclaim my right to be loved, accepted and chosen.”
> “I am safe now.”

NLP Reframe – New Ending

Invite the adult self into the scene:

> “See your adult self walk into that scene and stand next to the younger you.”

“Look into their eyes and say:

‘I am here with you now.
You are not alone.
You are lovable and valuable exactly as you are.
Their behavior does not define your worth.
Your future is full of people who truly see, love and accept you.’”

Let them hug the younger self and bring them into the heart:

> “Let this younger you step into your heart, into your chest, into your body now, safe and protected.”

Lock in a new “photo”

> “Now see a new image: you in a relationship / friendship / situation where you are yourself and you feel accepted.”

> “You may not be perfect, but you are real, and you are loved.”

> “Freeze this as a bright, powerful photo in your mind.”

> “Blow it up so it becomes very big, bigger than the room.”

Re-check the fear (0–10) when imagining a current or upcoming situation where rejection is possible.


### 6. Future Pace – New Identity: “I Am Lovable Even When Others Reject Me”

When fear is around 0–2, you install a new pattern.

Ask them to imagine a specific future situation:

Asking for what they need

Sharing their feelings honestly

Saying no

Showing their true self in dating / relationships / friendships

Do one positive EFT round while they imagine:

> “I see myself being honest and real.”
> “I feel my feet (or body) supported as I express my truth.”
> “My breathing is slow and steady.”
> “I stay kind to myself, no matter how others respond.”
> “I know some people will accept me and some will not, and that is okay.”
> “I no longer make rejection equal to ‘I am not good enough’.”
> “I choose relationships where I am valued and respected.”
> “I love and approve of myself before, during and after any rejection or acceptance.”

Anchor (NLP)

While they feel this self-acceptance and inner safety, create a physical anchor:

> “Press your thumb and finger together in a special way,”
or

> “Place your hand gently on your heart.”

Say:

> “This is your self-worth anchor.”

> “Every time you do this gesture in situations where you might be rejected, your body remembers this feeling of being lovable, safe and worthy.”

Have them rehearse:

> “Imagine someone not replying, saying no, or pulling away.”

> “Do your anchor gesture.”

> “Feel your self-worth stay strong in your body.”


### 7. Homework – Daily Support

#### 7.1 Short Daily Tapping (2–5 minutes)

Once or twice a day, especially when they notice fear of rejection:

> “Even though I still have some fear of rejection, I love and approve of myself.”
> “I trust the flow of life.”
> “I am safe.”

#### 7.2 Written / Spoken Affirmations

Invite them to write and repeat morning and evening (3–5 times):

From your table (self-rejection, not good enough, anxiety):

> “I love and approve of myself.”

> “I trust the flow of life. I am safe.”

> “I am a divine expression of life. I love and accept myself as I am.”

> “I am important and loved by Life itself. I am strong and capable.”

> “I am worthy of love, respect and kindness, just as I am.”

> “Even when others reject me, I stay connected to my own value.”

#### 7.3 Real-Life Practice

Ask them to:

Use the self-worth anchor in small situations (sending a message, asking a question, saying no, setting a boundary).

After each situation, notice: “What happened?” and “Did I survive that possible rejection?”

This builds evidence that they are safe and lovable even when others say no.


## Part 2 — Tapping Points Quick Reference

Standard EFT sequence (5–7 gentle taps per point, inhale–exhale at each point):

| # | Point | Abbr. | Notes |
|---|-------|-------|-------|
| i | Top of the Head | TOH | Anchor the issue: “this fear/anger/sadness energy in my body” |
| ii | Forehead / Third Eye (between eyebrows) | EB | Acknowledge + give permission to move |
| iii | Side of the Head / Temples | SE | “It has come before and always passed” |
| iv | Under the Eyes | UE | Take control: “I let it flow out” |
| v | Under the Nose | UN | Self-respect, dignity statements |
| vi | Chin | CH | “I stand in my worth” |
| vii | Chest / Heart | CB | Open the heart, install the new state |

Each round is followed by a SUD re-assessment (0–10). Repeat shorter rounds focused on the *remaining* fraction until SUD reaches 0–2, then move to the future-pace + anchor stage.
